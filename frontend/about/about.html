About Us

RoomMate Finder is an online platform that helps people find compatible roommates based on shared preferences, budget, and location. Whether you're moving to a new city or looking for someone to share rent, we're here to make it easier.

We believe in building a safe and trustworthy community where users can connect, communicate, and form lasting roommate relationships.

Launched in 2025, our mission is to simplify the roommate search process by offering intuitive tools, verified profiles, and a welcoming space for everyone.
